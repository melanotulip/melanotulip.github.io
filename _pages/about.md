I am a 2nd-year Ph.D. student in Statistics at [Rice University](https://statistics.rice.edu/). Prior to joining Rice, I received my B.S. in Statistics from the [School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University](https://www.math.pku.edu.cn/) in 2025. I also spent a semester as an exchange student at the [National University of Singapore](https://www.nus.edu.sg/) in Spring 2024.

Trained in mathematics and statistics, I am particularly drawn to the interplay between probability theory and statistical methodology. My research interests lie in **computational statistics, Bayesian methods, sampling algorithms, and network analysis**, spanning both the **theoretical foundations** of statistical computation and its **applications to modern machine learning**.

Outside of my research, I have a particular interest in ancient languages. I have been studying Ancient Greek for four years and recently began learning [Hittite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hittite_language). I also enjoy hiking, reading, music, and playing the guitar.
